iOS mobile app — StarSeat

01 Who publishes this app

StarSeat is developed and published by Anthony Raitif, an independent developer. The app is distributed on the App Store under the identifier com.antohny.toptable.

02 The principle: no data is sent

StarSeat does not ask you to create an account: no name, no email address, no phone number, no password. The app does not connect to any server: everything it does happens on your phone. No data is sent to the publisher or to any third party.

03 What the app stores on your phone

Only in the app's local storage, so it remembers you from one session to the next:

  • the language you chose;
  • your seating plans: the tables you filled and the names you placed on them.

This data never leaves your phone. It is deleted if you uninstall the app, or if you delete a plan in the app.

Sharing: when you share a table or a plan, the app turns it into an image and opens your phone's share sheet. You choose where to send it; the app transmits nothing.

Location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera: the app does not access any of them.

04 What we never do

  • Sell or pass on your data (we receive none).
  • Show advertising or include analytics or tracking tools.
  • Track you across apps or websites.
  • Collect an advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Ask you to create an account or to type free text: you only choose from a prepared list.

05 Third-party services

The app uses no third-party service that receives data from you. Fonts are bundled in the app. The share sheet and sending emails (suggestions, error reports, removal requests) use your phone's own features, only when you choose to.

06 Retention

Local data stays on your phone until you delete it or uninstall the app. If you write to us, we keep your message for as long as needed to handle your request.

07 Your rights

Since StarSeat collects no data on our servers, you stay in full control: you can erase a plan, or delete everything by uninstalling the app. For any question, write to us (see “Contact”).

08 Personalities mentioned and removal requests

The names of personalities in the list are mentioned for information only, from public sources. These personalities have no link with the app, did not approve it and do not endorse it. No photos of these people are used; the faces on the home screen are fictional characters. The choices shown in the plans are the players' own.

If you are a listed personality (or their representative) and want your name removed, write to us at the address below: it will be removed promptly.

09 Minimum age

StarSeat is not specifically aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data about them.

10 Changes to this policy

This policy may change if the app changes, for example if advertising were ever added. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.
